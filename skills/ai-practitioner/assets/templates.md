# Working templates

Use only the sections useful to the requested stage. Replace bracketed prompts with client facts or explicit unknowns. Keep real client files in an authorized private location outside this public toolkit.

## Client intake

```text
Client/problem (sanitized):
Who does this work and where:
Current trigger, steps, output, and pain:
Example of a good result:
Data sources and permissions known so far:
Required reads, writes, and approvals:
Constraints (stack, hosting, timing, volume, budget):
Evidence supplied:
What I want help deciding now:
```
