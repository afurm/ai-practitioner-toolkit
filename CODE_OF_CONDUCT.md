# Community conduct

Help people improve practical AI engineering decisions. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, spam, and disclosure of confidential information are not welcome. Do not post client records, credentials, or proprietary training materials.

The maintainer, [@afurm](https://github.com/afurm), may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate and consider context. For private concerns, ask for a private contact channel without posting sensitive details publicly; security vulnerabilities belong in the reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
